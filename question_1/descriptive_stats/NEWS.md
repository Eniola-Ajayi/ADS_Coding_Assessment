# descriptiveStats 0.2.0

* New `describe_stats()`: all of the package's statistics for the numeric
  columns of a data frame, overall or by one or more grouping columns (like
  `PROC MEANS` with a `CLASS` statement). Also accepts tibbles, data.tables
  and dplyr grouped data frames. The six vector functions are unchanged.

# descriptiveStats 0.1.0

* First version: `calc_mean()`, `calc_median()`, `calc_mode()`, `calc_q1()`,
  `calc_q3()` and `calc_iqr()`.
